A few common questions.

Where do you provide rental services?

We are based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and provide rental services across South India. Contact us with your venue to discuss logistics.

Can I rent individual items?

Share your item list and quantities. We can discuss equipment availability and a quotation tailored to your requirements.

Do you help with installation?

Our services include professional installation and on-site support. The scope and schedule are agreed as part of your event quotation.

How do I check prices and availability?

Call or email us with your date, location, guest count, and equipment needs. Prices and availability are confirmed directly by our team.
